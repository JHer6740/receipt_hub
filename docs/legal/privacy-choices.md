# Your privacy choices

**Draft — not for publication until legally reviewed.**

**Last updated:** {{EFFECTIVE_DATE}}

Everything you can do with your Receipt Hub information, and how. If something
here does not work, write to {{PRIVACY_EMAIL}} and we will do it for you.

You do not need the app to make a request, and we will not charge you for one.

---

## See your information

**In the app.** Your receipts, their line items and their photographs are all
visible: open **Receipts**, then any receipt.

Your account details are under **Account**.

Want everything in one place instead? Ask at {{PRIVACY_EMAIL}} and we will send
you a copy of what we hold about you.

---

## Export your information

**Account → Your data → Export this household.**

You get a CSV file with one row per line item, each row carrying the receipt it
came from: merchant, purchase date, receipt total, tax, status, then the line
item's description, quantity, unit, unit price, line total and category. It
opens in any spreadsheet.

The export covers the household you are currently in. If you are in more than
one, switch household and export again.

**Receipt photographs are not in the CSV.** To get copies of the images, ask at
{{PRIVACY_EMAIL}}.

---

## Correct your information

**Receipt details** — open the receipt, tap **Edit**. You can change the
merchant, date, total, tax, reference and every line item. Your correction
replaces what we read.

**Your name or email address** — not editable in the app yet. Write to
{{PRIVACY_EMAIL}} and we will change it.

**Household name** — an owner or admin can change it.

---

## Delete a single receipt

Open the receipt, then the menu, then **Delete**. It is removed from your
household's ledger and from its totals and insights.

This deletes it for **everyone in the household**, because the receipt belongs
to the shared ledger.

---

## Delete all of your receipt data

There is no single action for this yet — receipts are deleted one at a time.

If you want all of a household's receipts removed at once, write to
{{PRIVACY_EMAIL}} saying which household, and we will do it.

Deleting your account is a separate thing and does **not** delete the
household's receipts. See below.

---

## Delete your account

**Account → Your data → Delete my account**, or see
[Account deletion]({{PUBLIC_DOMAIN}}/account/delete).

Two things worth knowing before you do:

1. **It cannot be undone.** Export first if you want a copy.
2. **Receipts you filed stay with their household.** They belong to the shared
   ledger, not to you personally. If you want them gone too, delete them — or
   ask us to — *before* deleting your account.

---

## Leave a household

Ask an owner or admin to remove you, or write to {{PRIVACY_EMAIL}}.

You lose access immediately. Receipts you filed remain with the household.

---

## Withdraw consent

Anything you agreed to optionally, you can stop agreeing to. Write to
{{PRIVACY_EMAIL}} and say what you are withdrawing.

There is currently very little to withdraw: Receipt Hub has no analytics, no
advertising, no tracking, and no marketing email. The processing we do is what
is needed to run the account and household features you signed up for.

---

## Marketing preferences

**We do not send marketing email.** We have no email delivery at all at
present, so there is nothing to opt out of.

If that changes, it will be opt-in — we will ask before sending anything, and
every message will have a one-click unsubscribe.

---

## Object to or restrict processing

Depending on where you live, you may be able to object to certain processing or
ask us to restrict it. Write to {{PRIVACY_EMAIL}} explaining what you object to
and we will consider it and reply.

---

## Contact us, or complain

**{{PRIVACY_EMAIL}}** — for any request or complaint about your information. We
will acknowledge it and tell you what we are doing about it.

If you are not satisfied with our response you can go to a regulator:

- **Australia** — Office of the Australian Information Commissioner, oaic.gov.au
- **EU/EEA** — your national data protection authority
- **UK** — Information Commissioner's Office, ico.org.uk

You can go to a regulator without contacting us first, though we would rather
have the chance to put it right.

---

## What we never do

So you do not have to opt out of things that are not happening:

- We do not sell your information
- We do not use it for advertising, and there is no advertising in the app
- We do not track you across other apps or websites
- We do not send your receipts to any AI or OCR provider — reading them happens
  on our own server
- We do not share your receipts with other households
- We do not use your receipts to train models
