# Delete your Receipt Hub account

**Draft — not for publication until legally reviewed.**

**Last updated:** {{EFFECTIVE_DATE}}

This page explains how to delete your **Receipt Hub** account
({{PUBLIC_DOMAIN}}, Android package `com.receiptshub.receipts_hub`), operated by
{{OPERATOR_LEGAL_NAME}}.

You do **not** need the app to request deletion. Either route below works.

---

## Option 1 — request it here

Email **{{PRIVACY_EMAIL}}** from the address your account uses, with the subject
**Delete my account**.

We will confirm we received it, verify you control the address, and delete the
account. If we cannot verify it from the email itself, we will ask you one
question to confirm — we will never ask for your password.

**Timeframe:** we aim to complete deletion within 30 days of verifying the
request, and usually much sooner.

## Option 2 — in the app

**Account → Your data → Delete my account.**

You will be asked to confirm twice, the second time by typing `DELETE`, because
this cannot be undone.

---

## What gets deleted

- Your account record
- Your email address, display name and password hash
- Your membership of every household, and any outstanding requests to join one
- Your active sessions, so every signed-in device is signed out

## What is kept, and why

**Receipts you filed stay with their household.** This is the part people are
most often surprised by, so it is stated plainly:

A household is a shared ledger. When you file a receipt into one, it becomes
part of that household's records, which the other members rely on — their
totals, their trends and their shared history include it. Deleting your account
removes *you*, not the household's books.

If you want your receipts gone as well, do that **before** deleting your
account:

- Delete them individually in the app (open the receipt → menu → **Delete**), or
- Email {{PRIVACY_EMAIL}} and ask us to delete a household's receipts, or
- If you are the only member of the household, ask us to delete the whole
  household and we will remove it and everything in it

Also kept:

| What | Why | For how long |
|---|---|---|
| Backups containing your data | Backups are taken as whole snapshots and cannot be edited selectively | Until they rotate out — {{BACKUP_RETENTION}} |
| Server logs referencing your requests | Security and diagnostics | {{LOG_RETENTION}} |
| A minimal record that a deletion request was made and completed | To show we honoured it if asked | The period we are required to keep it |
| Anything the law requires us to retain | Legal obligation | Only as long as required, and only for that purpose |

Nothing kept for these reasons is used to provide the service or to contact you.

## Export your data first

Deletion is permanent. If you want a copy, export before you delete:

**Account → Your data → Export this household** gives you a CSV of every
receipt and line item in the household.

For copies of the receipt **photographs**, email {{PRIVACY_EMAIL}} before
deleting — we cannot retrieve them afterwards.

---

## If you own a household

If you are the owner of a household with other members, tell us what you want
to happen to it when you email us:

- **Delete it entirely**, with all of its receipts, or
- **Hand it over** to another member

Ownership cannot currently be transferred in the app, so we will do it for you.
If you delete your account without telling us, the household and its receipts
remain, but no one may be able to administer it — so it is worth a sentence in
your email.
